# Modern Fading & Seamless Blending

**SS Training | 10 CPD Hours**

## Purpose
Low, mid, high, taper and burst fades; guidelines, weight, transitions, detailing and head-shape control.

## Seven-part pathway
1. Professional context & goals
2. Core knowledge
3. Technique / strategy development
4. Applied decision-making
5. Modern practice
6. Activity & reflection
7. Final knowledge assessment

## Learning pattern
Each part can combine concise written knowledge, visual learning, a demonstration/explainer video, short knowledge checks and an application/reflection task. The final section contains the course assessment.

## Completion
Learners complete all required sections, achieve the configured pass mark and submit the required reflection/application evidence before the SS Training 10-hour CPD certificate is released.
