## Advanced learning stages

### 1. Audit your current working week

This stage focuses on audit your current working week. In modern barbering, technique should follow diagnosis rather than habit. The key principle here is to track a normal week before deciding what needs fixing.

Measure quality work completed within available time, not simply how fast you move. Rework, lateness and poor consultation are hidden productivity losses.

Productivity is useful output per constrained resource.

Use consultation, observation and controlled execution together. Think about the client's hair, head/face shape, routine, desired finish and maintenance. Make one deliberate decision at a time, then cross-check the result before making the next adjustment.

For CPD purposes, do more than remember a technique. Explain why it is suitable, recognise when it is not suitable, and be able to adapt it. That is the difference between copying a method and developing professional judgement.


**Professional evidence:** Professional application: using a real, recent or realistic case involving audit your current working week, identify the evidence you would gather, the main risk of making the wrong decision, the option you would choose, one credible alternative you would reject, and justify both decisions in at least 120 words.

### 9. Activity, reflection & final assessment

Professional CPD is strongest when knowledge changes what you do. Review the earlier stages and identify the decisions you will carry into real client work.

State whether the intervention worked, what evidence supports that conclusion and what you will do next.

Reflection should close the loop.

Your final assessment checks understanding across the course. The reflection is your evidence of application: be specific about what you will change, how you will measure it and what further development you may need.
