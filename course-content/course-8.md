## Advanced learning stages

### 1. Brand identity and target audience

This stage focuses on brand identity and target audience. In modern barbering, technique should follow diagnosis rather than habit. The key principle here is to a strong barber brand has a recognisable point of view, audience and standard of work.

Decide what type of work, experience and client you want to be known for. Visual style, captions, service delivery and response behaviour should reinforce the same position.

A personal brand is a repeated promise, not a logo.

Use consultation, observation and controlled execution together. Think about the client's hair, head/face shape, routine, desired finish and maintenance. Make one deliberate decision at a time, then cross-check the result before making the next adjustment.

For CPD purposes, do more than remember a technique. Explain why it is suitable, recognise when it is not suitable, and be able to adapt it. That is the difference between copying a method and developing professional judgement.


**Professional evidence:** Professional application: using a real, recent or realistic case involving brand identity and target audience, identify the evidence you would gather, the main risk of making the wrong decision, the option you would choose, one credible alternative you would reject, and justify both decisions in at least 120 words.
