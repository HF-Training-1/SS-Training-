MODERN FADING • MODULE 03

Read the weight.
Don't chase the line.

▶DEMONSTRATION VIDEOHigh-quality course video connects here
KNOWLEDGE

Control the transition.

A seamless fade depends on controlled removal of weight rather than repeatedly chasing every visible line. Step away from the client and identify whether you are seeing a true guideline, excess bulk or contrast created by density.

Your tool choice should respond to the problem. Corner work, controlled lever movement and clipper-over-comb can refine small areas while protecting the overall shape.

Before changing guard, ask: “What exactly am I trying to remove?”
QUICK CHECK

APPLY IT

Reflection task
